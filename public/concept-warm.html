Communities we serve

One district, many neighborhoods

District 11A covers all or parts of six communities in northwest Baltimore County, led by Owings Mills, Randallstown and Pikesville. Events, funding and resources here are for all of them.

  • Owings Mills
  • Randallstown
  • Pikesville
  • Reisterstown
  • Glyndon
  • Mays Chapel

Schools

  • Owings Mills High School
  • Randallstown High School
  • Pikesville High School
  • New Town High School

About Nico

Building coalitions that serve our community

I have worked at the intersection of housing, health, and community safety. I have helped organizations serve people with complex needs, and I have partnered with residents, service providers, and public agencies to deliver real outcomes.

This hub is where that work continues — a place to find real resources, hear what's happening around District 11A, and connect with the organizations already doing good work here.
